Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Operator: Before public launch, the site owner must replace this line with the legal name of the operator and a working privacy-contact email address.

Information this service handles

  • When you request a reading, your selected palm image, hand selection, reading options, language, and any optional notes are sent to this website's server. The server sends the image and prompt information to the Google Gemini API to generate the requested result.
  • When you use compatibility analysis, both selected palm images, the names you enter, and the selected language are sent to the server and Gemini.
  • The app saves up to three recent readings, including their images, in this browser's local storage. This data is stored on your device, not in an account. Use the app's history controls or clear this site's browser storage to remove it.
  • If you choose to create a share link, the reading and palm image are stored in the service database. Anyone who obtains the link can view them. Links expire after 30 days; while the reading remains open in the current browser session, you can revoke its link from the share dialog.
  • The hosting provider may process standard connection and operational logs, such as IP address, request time, and error details, to operate and protect the service.

Camera and image handling

Camera access is requested only after you choose the camera option and grant browser permission. Images are resized in your browser before upload. Do not upload another person's image unless you have permission. Do not include information in optional notes that you do not want processed by the service and its AI provider.

Service providers and international processing

Google processes submitted images and prompt information through the Gemini API. Google’s handling and retention may depend on the API product, account, and applicable terms. Review the current Google Gemini API terms and configure the appropriate account and data controls before launch. The pages also request fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google when those fonts load. The website's hosting and database providers may also process or back up data under their own terms and retention settings. Processing may occur in countries other than yours.

Retention and deletion

Reading images and results are not saved to a Palmora account by this app. They remain in your browser's local storage until removed there. Shared readings are deleted from the active application database when revoked or after the 30-day expiry. The hosting or database provider may retain backups according to its configured backup policy. Provider-side retention is governed by the applicable provider terms and settings.

Cookies, analytics, and security

The app does not currently implement an account, advertising tracker, or analytics product. It uses browser local storage for reading history. The service applies request limits and security headers, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and contact

You can decline camera permission and use an image file instead, avoid creating a share link, clear locally saved readings, and revoke an existing share link. For privacy questions or requests, contact the service operator using the privacy email published by the site owner before launch.

Changes

This notice should be updated if the service's providers, features, or data practices change. The site owner should have this notice reviewed for the jurisdictions where the service is offered.
